# Supplied coursework data

`z_boson_data_1.csv` and `z_boson_data_2.csv` are the two CSVs supplied with the owner's 2025 PHYS10362 project. Both are retained without modification, including malformed rows used by the original cleaning workflow.

Columns: centre-of-mass energy in GeV, cross section in nb, and its stated uncertainty in nb. The first line is the header. The files contain 103 data rows in total.

The source files do not identify a detector, experimental collaboration, simulation generator, or license. Treat the values as coursework inputs, not verified collider measurements. Original hashes and exclusion decisions are available in `../results/`.
